About Me

Behind the Lens

My name is Bao Hoang, and I am currently a college student pursuing a degree in Computer Science with a passion for photography and videography.

What started as a hobby through school classes has grown into a creative outlet that allows me to capture the world through my lens. My goal is to tell stories through visuals for both national and international audiences.

Whether it is through still images or cinematic films, I strive to create content that feels personal, emotional, and connected.

Portraits

Clean, expressive, and personal images that focus on mood and identity.

Events

Real moments captured with detail, timing, and atmosphere.

Video

Cinematic storytelling through movement, pacing, color, and composition.
